# Trademark Policy & Visual Identity Guidelines

This document establishes the ownership, permitted use boundaries, and legal protections governing the trademarks, product names, logos, and visual identity of the **Sharphy** project.

---

## 1. Ownership & Legal Status

The name **Sharphy**, the official Sharphy logo (`assets/sharphy.svg` and `assets/sharphy.png`), and related graphical branding are the exclusive intellectual property of **Bahar Maulidi**.

Although the Sharphy source code is released under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**, that license **explicitly does not grant trademark rights** (pursuant to Section 6 of Apache License 2.0: *"This License does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor..."*).

---

## 2. Permitted Uses

You are **PERMITTED** to use the "Sharphy" name without prior written consent strictly under the following conditions:

1. **Descriptive Attribution & Interoperability:**
   Accurately stating that your software, agent configuration, MCP client, or extension is compatible with or interfaces with Sharphy (e.g., *"MCP Client connector for Sharphy"*, *"Compatible with Sharphy v1.0"*).
2. **Linking to Official Resources:**
   Directing users to the official GitHub repository at [github.com/baharmaulidiii/sharphy](https://github.com/baharmaulidiii/sharphy) or official NuGet distribution packages (`Sharphy.Cli`, `Sharphy.Server`, `Sharphy.Core`).
3. **Academic Citations & Professional Credits:**
   Referencing participation in or contributions to the Sharphy project in academic papers, technical blogs, or professional portfolios.

---

## 3. Prohibited Uses

To prevent community confusion, safeguard project integrity, and protect security researchers from compromised imitations, the following actions are **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** without prior express written consent from Bahar Maulidi:

1. **Naming Forks or Derivative Projects:**
   If you fork or redistribute modified versions of this codebase, you **MAY NOT** name your derivative project in a manner that implies official endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation (e.g., naming your fork *"Sharphy Official"*, *"Sharphy Pro"*, or *"Sharphy Enterprise"* is prohibited).
2. **Binary Package Distribution Under Official Branding:**
   Distributing modified binaries on package managers (such as NuGet, Chocolatey, WinGet, or Homebrew) using official package identifiers (`Sharphy`, `Sharphy.Cli`, `Sharphy.Server`, `Sharphy.Core`) or the official brand logo is prohibited. Derivative distributions must use distinct namespaces (e.g., `MyOrg.SharphyFork.Cli`).
3. **Malicious Impersonation:**
   Using the Sharphy name, logo, or reputation to conceal malware, backdoors, or unauthorized cyber exploitation tools.
4. **Commercial Endorsement:**
   Using the Sharphy logo or brand to imply commercial certification or endorsement of third-party commercial services or products.

---

Violations of this trademark policy may result in immediate takedown requests (via GitHub DMCA/Trademark processes, NuGet administrative takedowns, or registrar notices) and applicable civil remedies.
